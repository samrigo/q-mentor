# Research Method

## Evidence card required fields

Claim · Source status · Boundary · Baseline · Artifacts · Limitations · Replication

## Source status labels

- peer-reviewed
- preprint
- official documentation
- vendor result
- secondary commentary

Never flatten them into equal evidence.

## Scoring (0–2 each)

Source rigor · Baseline strength · Artifact completeness · Scale relevance · Replication

Total is for triage only; weak evidence stays labeled `candidate`.
