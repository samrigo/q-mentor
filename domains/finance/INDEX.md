# Finance domain pack

First domain pack and reference demo. It refines vocabulary, toy models and
verdict examples; it never forks lifecycle schemas, provider contracts, consent
rules or evidence policy.

| Pack | Use when |
|---|---|
| [portfolio-optimization.md](portfolio-optimization.md) | Framing asset or bond selection as binary mean-variance optimization |
| [claims-and-limits.md](claims-and-limits.md) | Evaluating quantum-finance claims, vendor results, or "advantage" statements |

Fixture: `demo/bond-selection/` (six synthetic bonds). Not investment advice; no market or client data.
